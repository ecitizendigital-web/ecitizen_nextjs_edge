## Start with the business objective

If the goal is leads, measure lead actions. If the goal is ecommerce sales, measure purchases and revenue. If the goal is local discovery, measure calls, website visits and relevant local actions. The KPI hierarchy should follow the business model.

## Use a measurement stack

A modern agency website can combine GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel where relevant, Search Console and a lead endpoint or CRM. Each tool has a different role; installing every tool without a measurement plan only creates noise.

## Track meaningful events

Useful website events can include phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks, audit requests, contact submissions and important service CTA clicks. Event names should be consistent so reports remain understandable as campaigns grow.

## UTMs connect campaigns to outcomes

Campaign URLs can carry source, medium and campaign information so traffic from different marketing channels can be compared. UTM conventions should be standardized across the organization.

## Privacy and consent are part of the system

Tracking should be configured according to the relevant platform requirements and applicable privacy obligations. Measurement should not be treated as a reason to collect unnecessary personal data.

## Use data to improve decisions

The value of analytics is not the number of dashboards. It is the ability to answer questions such as: Which channel creates qualified leads? Which service page converts? Which content attracts relevant search traffic? Where does the enquiry journey fail?
