## What we ask for

Your name and phone number, your business name and type, an optional website or page link, what you want reviewed, your main goal and an optional message.

## Where it goes

When the form is connected to our lead service, your request is sent there directly. If that service is unavailable, the form opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

## How we use it

Only to contact you about your request and to prepare the audit or proposal you asked for.

## Your control

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time.
