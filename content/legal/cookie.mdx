## What is optional

- Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager: traffic and conversion measurement.
- Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel: campaign measurement.

None of these load until you accept, and only the tools we have switched on are used.

## What we store

One item in your browser records your choice (accepted or declined).

## Change your choice

Use <CookieSettingsButton /> to reopen the choice. If you decline after accepting, measurement stops from your next page load.
